What is an API exchange?

  • Your program sends a request.
  • Authentication identifies the caller.
  • The service returns a response or error.
  • Status and error details guide the next action.
  • Timeouts stop waiting forever.

What are we doing?

  • Verify the local environment and secret ignore rule.
  • Compile and inspect both Day 4 scripts.
  • Optionally inspect a live response.
  • Test every error-message branch locally.
  • Simulate a failed provider call safely.
  • Decide when retry is safe and idempotent.
  • Record evidence without secrets.

8. Check the retry decision

RETRY CHECKLIST
Write-Output "Retry only if all are true:"
Write-Output "1. The failure may be temporary."
Write-Output "2. Repeating the operation is safe or idempotent."
Write-Output "3. A timeout and maximum attempt count exist."
Write-Output "4. Backoff is used for 429, 5xx, or connection failures."
Write-Output "5. Final failure has a clear fallback or escalation."

What this doesPrints the decision gate used before adding retries.

Why we do itRetries are an operational decision. Repeating a permanent error or a side effect can increase load, cost, or duplicate actions.
